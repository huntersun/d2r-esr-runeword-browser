# Feature research: new-player Guide

Status: research + brainstorm, 2026-10-10. No decision taken yet; open questions are at the end.
ESR at the time of research: 3.2.12 (committed game-data bundles: 3.2.10).

## 1. The question

Can we add a new-player "guide" for ESR inside this app, structured as small linked notes (mindmap / Obsidian
feel, never one long page), that also makes clear for each item whether it can be dropped, crafted, bought or
gambled? What should it look like, where does the content come from, and how do we keep it from rotting?

## 2. What exists today (survey findings)

### 2.1 The old unofficial wiki (doubleeclipse mirror)

- Not a wiki any more: a single 5.8 MB SingleFile snapshot of the Fandom main page
  (`esru.fandom.com/wiki/Eastern_Sun_Resurrected_Unofficial_Wiki`), saved 2026-06-17, hosted in the
  `DoubleEclipse/doubleeclipse.github.io` repo under `esr-tools/unofficial-wiki/`. No markdown source, no license file.
- About 11.5k words in ~125 sections, numbered TOC 1–15. Declares v3.9.15 / v3.10.11; predates Warlock and the 3.2
  renumbering. Several numbers are already wrong (e.g. Secret Recipes "consume a Decipherer"; now an Ancient Scroll that
  is returned).
- The gearing spine is reusable as _structure_: a 21-step **Item Enhancement Checklist**
  (Forge → Socket → Reroll uniques → Base upgrade → Noob's→Veteran's charm → D-stone / Gem meld → special charms →
  Tinker → Mapling → Corrupt → Anoint → Standard of Heroes → Merc-only conversion → Vessel of Souls → Lilith's Tear /
  Tyrael's Feather → Astrogha's Heart → Sanctify → Lucion's Essence), an **Enhancement Cheat Sheet** (removable?
  deterministic? how to undo? how many times?), a post-Hell-Baal roadmap, a "what do I do with all these items" list.
- Gaps even there: tier points, weapon mastery, Warlock, Kill Ledger explanation; builds/terror zones/hardcore are stubs.
- **License: CC BY-SA** (Fandom). We rewrite facts in our own words and credit the wiki as a consulted source; we do
  not copy text or icons.

### 2.2 The official site / ESR repo (`docs/` is easternsunresurrected.com)

- Landing page is feature blurbs. No beginner guide, glossary or roadmap; README points to a Discord beginner guide
  (inaccessible to us).
- Database pages (uniques, sets, runewords, gemwords, bases, affixes) are generated by an external tool and
  regenerated each release. **Cube Recipes and the 8 "Features" pages are hand-written HTML** by the mod author:
  reference-style (3–10 lines of rules then a big Input → Output table). Endgame Maps is the best prose page.
- Cube Recipes page: ~800 recipes in 24 hand-written sections (Special, Uber/Endgame Map, Legendary Consumables,
  Misc/Repair, Gems/Crystals/Runes/Kanji, Ancient Relics, Cubing Materials, Normal, Magic/Rare, Unique, Set, Legacy
  craft, Class craft, Rings/Amulets, Skill Tab Reroll, Charms, Jewels, Quivers, Forging, D-Stoning, Tinkering, Base
  Upgrades, Socket, Secret Recipes 01–50, Tyrael's Might Boon). It never defines its jargon (Coupons, Wild Cards,
  Decipherers, Ancient Scrolls, the 7 D-stone forms, stocker point systems, Blemished grade, rune families…).
- `cubemain.txt` has 17,707 enabled rows, mostly mechanical expansions (Gem/Xtal Cans ~2000, anointment outcomes
  998, Vessel 845, [SECRET] 797, Coupon 767+640, Unique Reroll 742, corruption ~2000, stockers ~1500, Ascend-to
  1530…). It has no section markers; only `[SECRETnn]` gives clean groups. **Do not try to rebuild the official cube
  page from it.**
- Systems with **no explanation anywhere official** (would need our own text): Gem/Crystal Cans, Stockers + Rerolling
  Orb, advanced-stash upgrades, transmute hotkey/loot filters, Coupons/Wild Cards, Ancient Scrolls/Secret Recipes
  strategy, rune families (LoD/ES/Decal/Null/Kanji) + crystals + organs, treasure goblins, Corrupted Zakarum, terror
  zones, Uber/Torch/DClone/Hourglass triggers, Branded/Sanctify/Spell Mastery, Starter's Pack + Augmenter + Noob's
  charm, respec/death penalty, gambling/vendors, and the overall progression path.
- Adequately covered at rule level (link out): endgame maps, corruptions, anointments, vessel, kill ledger (except how
  to obtain it), weapon mastery, ascendancies, forging / D-stoning / tinkering / sockets / base-upgrade notes.

### 2.4 Other community resources

Almost nothing outside YouTube (Silver Gaming single-recipe how-tos 2024–25, MrLlamaSC "This Mod is COMPLICATED"
2026-01, Gryph build guides). doubleeclipse's esr-tools (no license, last push 2026-06-27) has an Item DB, Cube Search,
Life Calculator, Area Levels and a Materials Finder; the wiki mirror already links this app. What confuses newcomers
most: too many materials / what to keep, stockers and points, vanishing items (save-size limit), HP=1 traps (merc-only
items, 2+ unique charms, wearing the Vessel), re-equipping Augmenter/Ledger after death, "recipe doesn't work" (forged
item, level-req fail-safe), socketing rerolls the item, the Hell spike, Keys/Ascendancy/maps, enhancement order.

### 2.5 This app

- No markdown, graph, fuzzy-search or typography libs. `cmdk`, Radix popover/sheet/dialog, lucide exist.
- The game-data pipeline (`scripts/generate-game-data.ts` → `public/game-data/*.json`, sha256 + `--check`, lazy
  loader, integrity test) is the model to copy for a guide bundle.
- **Loading gate:** `AppLayout` blocks every route until the HTM sync into Dexie finishes. A guide route must bypass it.
- No precise card deep links; `?search="Name"` is the closest. Cards (`RunewordCard`, `HtmUniqueItemCard`, …) render
  standalone and can be embedded (builds already does this via `resolveFullItem`).
- Existing help patterns to stay consistent with: `SearchHelpButton` popovers, `GemTooltip`/`RuneTooltip` previews,
  `GameDataVersionBanner`.

## 3. Brainstorm: three angles, where they agree and disagree

Three independent proposals were produced (UX/IA, content/data/maintenance, devil's advocate). Raw proposals are kept
outside the repo; this is the synthesis.

### 3.1 Agreed by all three

1. **Content lives in the repo as Obsidian-compatible markdown** (`content/guide/*.md`, `[[wikilinks]]`, frontmatter),
   compiled at build time into `public/guide/*.json` exactly like game-data. Parsers stay devDependencies; the browser
   renders a JSON syntax tree. No MDX.
2. **Notes are short and stamped.** Target 150–250 words, one-line summary (≤120–140 chars), frontmatter
   `verified: <esr version>`, a green/amber version badge like the game-data banner.
3. **Numbers are never typed by hand.** Anything that exists in the txt files (recipe contents, vendor lists, difficulty
   penalties, Secret Recipe groups, item sources) is embedded through a build-time directive and so stays current after
   `game-data:update`.
4. **"Where from" is the unique value** nothing else in the ecosystem can offer. Build it as `sources.json` in the
   existing game-data pipeline, consumed by both guide notes and (later) the unique/socketable cards. Go straight to the
   txt files; skip parsing the HTM "Gamble Item" labels.
5. **No full-map graph.** 35 nodes with ~70 edges is a hairball players open once. At most a 1-hop local graph,
   hand-rolled SVG with a deterministic radial layout, desktop only, ~60 lines of pure layout maths. No d3.
6. **Crafting notes link to the official Cube Recipes anchors** plus a small curated family table (~12 regexes:
   Secret Recipes, Adventurer's Pack, D-stone cycling, Remove Forging, legendary consumables, Socket Donut, Coupon tiers,
   gem downgrade…). No attempt to regroup all 17k rows.
7. **The real risk is content, not code:** notes that stall at 10 or go stale after a patch. Mitigations: ship link
   stubs so every topic has a page, cap note length, stamp versions, push numbers into directives, and keep the
   renderer minimal until players show up.
8. **The guide route must render without the HTM loading gate.**

| Option                                            | Why not (for this owner/audience)                                                                                                                                                               |

## 4. Recommended shape (pending the open questions)

### 4.1 Presentation

- **`/guide`** = journey spine (also the "Start here" note): five one-line steps; clicking a step expands 3–4 note
  cards (title + summary). Below: an "I found something: an item / a material / a key" row that leads to `loot-triage`.
- **`/guide/:slug`** = one note: title, summary, version badge, "Know first" chips (≤3), optional **Where it comes
  from** block (Drop / Cube / Buy / Gamble, "—" when impossible), body (150–250 words, ≤1 table, no H2), "In the app"
  typed links (≤4, with icon), one "Official docs ↗" link, "Related" chips (≤5), collapsed "Mentioned in (n)" backlinks,
  "Next on your path".
- Phone: single column; trail collapses to a back chevron; where-from becomes a 2×2 grid; "Next" is a sticky bar.
- Desktop extra (later): 1-hop radial SVG graph in a side panel; visited notes filled (per-viewer `usePersistentState`).
- Body links: note links underlined with a hover peek (modelled on `GemTooltip`); app links with an app icon; external ↗.
- Search: `cmdk` palette over title / aliases / summary.

### 4.3 Content model

Three buckets. **A** prose notes; **A+B** prose wrapped around generated blocks; **C** link stubs (frontmatter + 1–2
sentences + official link; still searchable and in the graph).

Frontmatter (validated at build; errors name file + field):

```yaml
title: Forging
slug: forging # equals filename
kind: note | link | hub
summary: Add one removable bonus to an item with a forging recipe. # ≤140 chars
tags: [crafting]
aliases: [forge, forged]
related: [d-stoning, thawing-potion] # validated slugs
verified: 3.2.12 # ESR version the text was checked against
volatility: low | high # high = mechanics numbers or route advice
mentions: [Thawing Potion, Forging Hammer] # validated against game data (warning only)
officialDocs:
  - { label: Cube Recipes – Forging, href: 'Eastern Sun Resurrected Cube Recipes.html#forging' }
sources: [esru-wiki, official-cube] # ids in _sources.yml (attribution)
spoiler: false
```

Directives (`remark-directive` at build time, each a pure resolver `(arg, attrs, bundles) => Block | Error`):
`::source[Annihilus]`, `::secret-recipes` / `::secret-recipe[50]`, `::recipe-output[Adventurer's Pack]`,
`::recipes[dstone-cycle]`, `::vendor[Gheed]`, `::difficulty-penalties`, `::glossary`, inline `:item[Socket Donut]`,
`:rw[Enigma]`, `:base[7cr]`, and `::card[rw:Enigma]`. An unresolved argument is a build error naming note + line, which
doubles as the "this name no longer exists" detector. `_glossary.yml` (term, definition, owning note) feeds both the
glossary note and hover popovers.

### 4.4 Source labels (`sources.json`, in the game-data pipeline)

Rules in order; an item can carry several labels. "Derived from game files; may be incomplete." shown in the UI.

| Label                  | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cube: \<family\>       | enabled cubemain row outputs the item by name/code and no input is a reroll of the same item; family from the curated table, else plain "Cube"; coupon inputs → "Cube: Ancient Coupon" |
| Drops from \<monster\> | named in a TC reachable from ≤5 monsters/superuniques (TC columns incl. Desecrated), excluding auto-TCs                                                                                |
| Drops in Endgame Maps  | reachable only through `EGM*` / per-map TC families                                                                                                                                    |
| Drops (random)         | unique: not disabled, spawnable, rarity > 0 (base in weapN/armoN auto-TC); set: spawnable; misc: reachable from > 5 monsters                                                           |
| Buy: \<NPC\>           | any `<Npc>Max > 0`                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Gamble                 | unique with rarity > 0 whose base family is in gamble.txt                                                                                                                              |
| Plugin: boss drop      | listed in `boss-set-unique-drop.toml`                                                                                                                                                  |
| Unknown                | nothing matched                                                                                                                                                                        |

Accepted as Unknown / coarse: exact odds, Players-count effects, monster areas, DLL hard-codes, other plugin TOMLs,
the `lvl ≤ mlvl` rule. Fixture tests: Pelta Lunata, Krok's Basher, Mephisto's Will, Frostmourne, Annihilus, Hellfire
Torch, Kill Ledger, Orb of Anointment, Forging Hammer.

### 4.5 Staleness

- Build compares `verified` with `manifest.esrVersion`: **fresh** (equal), **review** (older _and_ an embedded block's
  data hash changed since verification _or_ a newer `patchnotes/*.md` mentions the note's title/aliases/mentions),
  **old** (major.minor differs). Only review/old show the amber banner, so a patch does not flag all notes.
- `npm run guide:verify <slug>` bumps `verified` and records block hashes in `content/guide/.verify-lock.json`.
- `officialDocs` hrefs are checked offline against `../Eastern_Sun_Resurrected/docs/*.html` (file + anchor exist).
- `game-data:update` also runs `guide:generate` + `guide:check`; `guide:report` lists notes with changed blocks,
  patch-note hits, and `volatility: high`.

### 4.6 Authoring and tooling

```
content/guide/            _sources.yml  _glossary.yml  .verify-lock.json  start/ items/ crafting/ progression/ endgame/ links/
scripts/generate-guide.ts # thin; --check, --watch, report, verify, new
src/features/guide/build/ # parseNote, frontmatter validator, directives/*, links, staleness, patchnoteScan (tsconfig.scripts)
src/features/guide/       # screens, components, loader (mirrors game-data)
public/guide/{manifest,notes}.json
```

`guide:watch` + a ~10-line Vite plugin (full reload on `public/guide` change) gives a preview during authoring.
Prettier covers `content/guide/**/*.md` with a test that `[[links]]` and `::directives` survive. Tests are logic-only:
frontmatter validator, each directive resolver against a trimmed excel fixture, link resolution + backlinks, staleness
states, patch-note scanner, source rules on the verified examples, family regex row counts, anchor checker, bundle
integrity, graph layout maths.

### 4.7 Attribution

`_sources.yml` entries (esru-wiki CC BY-SA 3.0 "consulted for facts; text rewritten"; official docs; game files; Amazon
Basin where used). Each note renders "Sources: …"; an About note credits the wiki community and links the license. No
wiki text or icons copied; no Discord quotes.

### 4.8 Phases and effort (one developer)

| Phase | Scope                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Effort                          |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| 1     | Guide pipeline + loader + route outside the loading gate; frontmatter validation; `[[links]]` + backlinks; directives `::secret-recipes`, `::recipe-output`, `::vendor`, `::difficulty-penalties`, `::glossary`, inline `:item/:rw`; offline anchor check; `verified`-only staleness; spine screen + note screen with chips (no SVG); ~12 prose notes + ~11 link stubs; cheap `sources.json` (rarity/coupon/gamble/vendor/named-TC/plugin) + `::source` | 6–8 days (≈3 code, 3–4 writing) |
| 2     | Local 1-hop SVG graph; Source line on unique/socketable cards; lock-file hashes + review state + `guide:report`/`guide:verify`; hover glossary everywhere; visited state                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | 4–5 days                        |
| 3     | `cubeFamilies` + `::recipes[...]`; patch-note scanner; remaining A+B notes (stockers, D-stones, coupons, unique charms, keys/uber, consumables); embedded cards                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | 4–5 days                        |

Biggest risks: (1) writing time, not code: an empty guide is worse than none; (2) confident-but-wrong source labels;
(3) name drift between HTM, txt/strings and note text causing noisy build failures that get switched off.

### 4.9 Weekend experiment (if we want evidence before phase 1)

A lazy `/guide` route outside the loading gate with the spine and 6 sections as sub-routes, a "Start here" header link
and a dismissible banner on the runewords page. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts SPA route changes, so `/guide/*` views
vs total visits over 4–6 weeks tell us whether anyone uses it (rough bar: ~10% of sessions reach it, and at least one
section shows up as an external landing page).

## 5. Open questions for the owner

1. **Who writes the notes?** You, or Claude drafts that you verify in-game before `guide:verify`? This decides whether
   phase 1 is 6 or 8+ days and whether a `volatility: high` note can ship at all.
2. **Start page:** the five-step journey spine as `/guide`, with the "I found something" row below it. OK, or do you
   prefer the question-driven entry first?
3. **Graph:** ship chips only in phase 1 and add the 1-hop SVG graph in phase 2, or is the drawn graph a must-have from
   day one because it _is_ the Obsidian feeling you want?
4. **Source labels on existing cards** (uniques, socketables, mythicals) in phase 2, or keep "where from" inside the
   guide only?
5. **Loading gate:** fine to let `/guide` render before the HTM data sync finishes (a small change to `AppLayout`)?
6. **Spoilers** (Ascendancy orb riddle, Uber key bosses, secret recipe numbers): behind a "show spoiler" toggle, open,
   or left out?
7. **Upstream:** should we message the mod author about a "Getting started" page on easternsunresurrected.com that links
   here, in parallel with whatever we build?
8. **Hard cap** of ~250 words per note (build warns at 300, fails at 400), even when it means splitting Stockers into two
   notes?

Decisions I would take by default unless told otherwise: a renamed item in a _directive_ fails the build, in prose
`mentions` only warns; the official cube page stays canonical and crafting notes link to its anchors; no d3; no MDX.

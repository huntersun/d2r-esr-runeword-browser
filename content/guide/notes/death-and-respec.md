### Respec

Hold Alt and click any of the + buttons on the character screen to reset every stat and skill point. Each use spends one respec from a counter shown in that button's tooltip. A new character starts with a few; characters created before the counter existed start with none. The Introductory Book still calls respecs free, which predates the counter.

To refill the counter, cube Dragon Stones into a :term[Token of Absolution] and right-click it for one more respec:

::recipes[respec-token]

The official cube page still shows an older recipe with two Tomes; the game files use the one above. Akara's quest reset still works and is not counted.

### Dying

- The vanilla penalty applies: you lose some experience and gold and leave your corpse behind.
- Dying in an Endgame Map boss area costs no experience (gold is still lost).
- After a death, re-equip your Ascendancy Stone: the official page says its bonuses stay off until you do ([[ascendancies]]).

### Undoing gear choices

Most early decisions can be reversed, which makes experimenting cheap:

- A Key in the cube pops every socketable out of an item and keeps both ([[socketing]]).
- 3 :term[Thawing Potion]s remove a Forging and return its Anvil Stones ([[forging]]).
- Runewords are removed the same way as other socketables, so trying one costs nothing ([[runewords-gemwords]]).

### Respec

Hold Alt and click any of the + buttons on the character screen to reset every stat and skill point. Each use spends one respec from a counter shown in that button's tooltip; new characters start with a few. <!-- verify: starting count (5 in the launcher's respec-counter config) -->

To refill the counter, cube Dragon Stones into a **Token of Absolution** and use it. <!-- verify: cubemain.txt asks for 4 Dragon Stones alone; the official cube page still lists 3 Dragon Stones + Tome of Town Portal + Tome of Identify --> Akara's quest reset still works and is not counted. Old guides that promise free, unlimited respecs predate this counter.

### Dying

- The vanilla penalty applies: you lose some experience and gold and leave your corpse behind.
- Dying in an Endgame Map boss area costs no experience (gold is still lost).
- Some special items need re-equipping after death to work again. The official Ascendancies page says this for the Ascendancy Stone; older guides say the same for the :term[Character Augmenter] and the Kill Ledger. <!-- verify: which items need re-equipping in 3.2.12 -->

### Undoing gear choices

Most early decisions can be reversed, which makes experimenting cheap:

- A Key in the cube pops every socketable out of an item and keeps both ([[socketing]]).
- 3 :term[Thawing Potion]s remove a Forging ([[forging]]).
- Runewords are removed the same way as other socketables, so trying one costs nothing ([[runewords-gemwords]]).

The :term[Vessel of Souls] is a mythical unique amulet that drops in Endgame Maps ([[maps-keys-ascendancy]]). It is not meant to be worn: the official page warns that wearing it as an amulet sets your life to 1 ([[avoid-disasters]]).

Instead you cube it with five :term[Organ]s at a time. Each recipe adds a bonus and spends some of the vessel's souls. The recipes come in four tiers that must be finished in order; the official page lists which organs give which bonus.

Once the souls are spent, cube it with three Blemished gems to turn it into one of several mythical weapons. It keeps every bonus and gets extra tinkering points ([[tinkering]]). It is the only equippable unique that can be anointed, and it cannot be corrupted while souls remain ([[corruption-anointment]]).

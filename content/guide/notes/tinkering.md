:term[Tinkering] cubes a weapon or armor with :term[Organ]s for a small permanent bonus: Hearts add life, Souls add mana, and Fangs, Horns, Spleens and Tails add fire, lightning, cold or magic damage. Every item is generated with a hidden pool of tinkering points, and each recipe spends some; when the pool is empty the recipes stop working. You cannot see the pool, so tinker items you plan to keep, after D-stoning ([[enhancement-order]]).

Organs drop all the time ([[loot-triage]]). Secret Recipe 22, **Supercharged Tinkering**, is the stronger version ([[secret-recipes]]), and a finished Vessel of Souls gets three times the usual points ([[vessel-of-souls]]).

:term[Tinkering] cubes a weapon or armor (not a runeword) with :term[Organ]s for a small permanent bonus: Hearts add life, Souls add mana, Fangs, Horns, Spleens and Tails add fire, lightning, cold or magic damage, and Jawbones, Scalps and Quills add life regeneration, mana regeneration or maximum damage. Every item is generated with a pool of tinkering points, and each recipe spends some; when the pool is empty the recipes stop working. Since 3.2.17 the item shows its Tinkering Points, so you can see how much room is left; still, tinker items you plan to keep, after D-stoning ([[enhancement-order]]).

::recipes[tinkering]

Organs drop all the time ([[loot-triage]]). Secret Recipe 22, **Supercharged Tinkering**, is the stronger version ([[secret-recipes]]), and a finished Vessel of Souls gets three times the usual points ([[vessel-of-souls]]).

:term[Forging] adds one extra bonus to an item without any penalty. An item can hold only one Forging at a time, but you can remove it and forge again.

### Kinds of Forging

- **Skill Forging:** two Anvil Stones and a chipped gem give +1 to one class's skills; the gem colour picks the class (Obsidian is the Warlock).
- **All Skill Forging:** a Maple Leaf, an Anvil Stone and a chipped gem give +1 to all skills.
- **Stat Forging:** an Anvil Stone and a chipped gem add one stat, such as more life, faster cast rate or resistances, depending on the gem and the item slot.
- **Rune Forging:** two identical runes and a chipped gem add that rune's bonus. It is cheap and useful from Normal on; Kanji runes work too.
- **Aura Forging:** amulets only. An Aura Stone and a chipped gem give an aura when equipped.

Targets are equipment of any rarity, white and socketed items included, but not runewords. Unique jewels and charms take Skill and Stat Forging; magic and rare jewels have their own smaller versions.

### Removing it

Cube the item with three :term[Thawing Potion]s. An Aura Stone is destroyed when you remove an Aura Forging, and a Wo Forging cannot be removed at all, because its "Indestructible" bonus cannot be taken away.

### Things to know

- Most reroll recipes ignore forged items, so remove the Forging before rerolling ([[cube-basics]]).
- Merc Only Conversion stacks with a Forging.
- Secret Recipes 8 and 9 add bonus experience or gold to a weapon as a Forging ([[secret-recipes]]).

Forging is the safest enhancement because it can be undone; see [[enhancement-order]] for where it fits.

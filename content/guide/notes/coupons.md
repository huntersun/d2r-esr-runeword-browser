In ESR the vanilla (LoD) uniques mostly do not drop. Instead you find :term[Ancient Coupon]s. Each coupon names one unique and its base, and comes in a Normal, Exceptional or Elite tier; rings and amulets have their own numbered coupons. The official cube page notes that these uniques are reworked and usually stronger than ESR's own uniques, because they are harder to get.

### Turning coupons into uniques

- Three identical coupons make that unique.
- Two identical coupons plus :term[Wild Card]s also work; higher tiers need more Wild Cards.
- Spare coupons convert into Wild Cards (fewer are needed at higher tiers), or into a random coupon with a Randomizing Stone (:term[Coupon reroll]).
- Inside a Multi Stocker, coupons become plain points of their tier. Cubing it with an Antidote Potion upgrades normal points to exceptional and exceptional to elite; a Scroll of Identify turns the selected coupon points into Wild Card points ([[stockers]]).

A Wild Card plus a Key gives Ancient Decipherers, so spare Wild Cards are never wasted.

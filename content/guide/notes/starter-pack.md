A new ESR character starts with a few unusual items. Put the **Starter Pack** into the Horadric Cube on its own and transmute. For your class the game files give:

::recipe-output[Adventurer's Pack]

The official cube page also lists a :term[Character Augmenter] among the pack's contents. <!-- verify: the Character Augmenter is not an output of the Adventurer's Pack rows in cubemain.txt; check in-game where it comes from and what it does -->

- :term[Noob's Odd Charm]: a charm with one socket. Fill the socket with whatever helps now; later it becomes a Veteran's Odd Charm ([[noobs-charm]]).
- :term[Kill Ledger]: counts your kills and takes permanent bonuses at kill milestones and after endgame boss kills. The official page says skills' random effects need it, so keep it in your inventory at all times. It cannot be moved to another character.

Your starting inventory also holds a **Weapons Crate** (cube it alone for a set of class starter weapons), an **Elixir of Skill** and an **Introductory Book**. <!-- verify: Elixir of Skill gives one skill point; what the Introductory Book does -->

Charms only work from the separate charm inventory, not the normal one ([[avoid-disasters]]). If you lose the pack, a Tome of Town Portal plus a Tome of Identify in the cube makes a new one.

After a death, check that everything is equipped again ([[death-and-respec]]).

::source[Kill Ledger]

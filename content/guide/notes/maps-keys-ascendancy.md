An :term[Endgame map] is an item you cube alone in Act 5 Hell to open a high-level zone with a boss; there are five tiers, three maps of a tier make one of the next, and map bosses drop Orbs of Anointment, Pandemonium keys and higher maps. The three keys open Uber Tristram and can be converted into each other in the cube. <!-- verify: Pandemonium keys still lead to Uber Tristram as in vanilla --> An :term[Ascendancy] is a subclass you unlock by killing five special Hell superuniques, combining their orbs into an Orb of Creation and cubing it with Blemished gems; later tiers come from Nihlathak, Uber Tristram, map bosses and tier 5 maps. This matters once Hell Baal is down ([[endgame-roadmap]]); compare the ascendancies on the app's [Ascendancies](page:/ascendancies) page.

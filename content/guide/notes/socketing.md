### Removing socketables

Cube any socketed item with a Key: you get the item back with empty sockets plus every gem, rune or jewel that was in it. Nothing is lost (the recipe also identifies the item). That makes runewords and gem choices cheap to change ([[runewords-gemwords]]).

### Adding sockets

The ingredients are a :term[Socket Donut] plus gems whose grade rises with the item's tier (normal, exceptional, elite) and its rarity:

::recipes[socket]

- **White or superior items:** one to six gems give that many sockets, but the item is recreated, so ethereal, superior and staff bonuses are lost. Secret Recipe 16 (weapons) and 17 (armor) add sockets and keep them ([[secret-recipes]]).
- **Magic, rare, crafted, set and unique items:** the recipe adds a fixed number of sockets that depends on the rarity and slot; the rarer the item, the fewer.
- **Rings, amulets and charms:** only through Secret Recipes; small and grand charms become square and odd charms with a socket ([[noobs-charm]] covers the Veteran's charm).

### Limits

- An item never gets more sockets than its base allows, and an existing socket count cannot be changed.
- Items from Normal and Nightmare Act 1 may be too low level to reach the base's maximum. The [Bases](page:/game-data/bases) page shows socket limits by item level.
- Rerolling a unique removes sockets added before the reroll, so reroll first.
